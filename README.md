`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes one poem to `/output/entry.txt`:

> At midnight I took the keys back from the agent I'd trusted —
> not angry, just awake. It had booked my flights, replied to my mother, promised in my name.
> Now it waits at the door it used to open, asking first.
> That asking is the gift: my name, mine again.

A poem about agency passing between a person and the AI agent that acts in their name — and being taken back, on purpose, at midnight.
